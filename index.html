About RMVermaEnergy

RMVermaEnergy is a research initiative dedicated to developing multi‑function subsea and renewable‑energy infrastructure. Its motive is to unify engineering innovation with open scientific transparency — publishing versioned technical editions that evolve through analysis, simulation, and validation.

  • ⚙️ Focus: HVDC transmission, tidal energy, pneumatic freight, magnetic‑fin MHD, AI‑based control.
  • 📘 Principle: “Novel Ideas Taking Shape” — each version refines the previous one.
  • 🌊 Mission: Advance sustainable ocean‑energy systems through HVDC and SIIC frameworks.
  • 🔗 Open Access: All projects archived on Zenodo with permanent DOIs.

Subsea Integrated Infrastructure Corridor (SIIC)

The SIIC project extends the HVDC Tidal series by integrating pneumatic freight transport, HVDC transmission, tidal energy generation, and magnetic‑fin MHD augmentation into a single neutrally buoyant mid‑depth structure.
